---
title: Archimedes
description: Ships you can stand on, built from ordinary blocks on a Paper server.
---

Archimedes takes a block build and lifts it off the world. The blocks you assembled from are gone; what you see is a moving picture of the hull, and what you stand on is a solid deck. Wool becomes cloth that billows in the wind. The ship is still there after a restart.

These pages are for players. Start with [ships](/docs/ships) if you want to assemble something you already built, or [sailing](/docs/sailing) if you want a ready-made hull with a sail.
