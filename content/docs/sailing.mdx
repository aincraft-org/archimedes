## The idea: cloth that catches the air

A sail on Archimedes is wool (or a banner) treated as **cloth**, not as another hull cube. Still air keeps a thin sheet. Wind cups the sheet so the plates turn and shrink like fabric, while the mast stays put. Each wool block is a square metre of pressure sail. You do not need to tessellate anything yourself — the plugin draws the cloth.

You can assemble a sail you built, as in [ships](/docs/ships), or spawn a ready-made demo hull with a command.

## The basics: what you need on day one

Face open space and run `/arch sail`. A medium oak deck, mast, and white wool wall appear three blocks in front of you. The default is a 5×5 deck with a 5×5 sail. The command does not scan a build and does not clear the world first. If the hull cannot rise out of the ground, it still stays in the world.

You must be a player. Permission failures are explicit. If spawn is refused, you see `Cannot spawn sail:` plus the reason.

## Everyday: what you can do

`/arch sail small`, `/arch sail medium`, and `/arch sail large` pick the hull size (3×3, 5×5, and 7×7 decks and sails). `/arch sail mesh` (or `small mesh`, `large-mesh`, and the same idea for the other sizes) builds a thicker cloth volume so the sail occupies real depth, not only a one-block wall.

Stand on the deck and wait for wind. A 1-block-thick wall stays a sheet in still air; breeze billows the plates. `/arch inspect` from [ships](/docs/ships) is how you read the sampled wind and the sail force. Same-facing cloth collapses to one vector in that report.

If you captured a dispenser with a stone button on it, that button is the cannon. The owner or an operator right-clicks it to fire a visible, non-incendiary shot from the current muzzle. There is a two-second cooldown, no ammunition, and no special item model. Anyone else is told they cannot fire it.

## Advanced: torn cloth, and what the picture is made of

The server cannot upload a custom 3D model. Hull blocks stay one untransformed cube each. Cloth is many thin plates. If cloth tears, the torn piece becomes a ragdoll that keeps moving with the ship, and the remaining sail is redrawn from what is still intact. Torn marks are not saved; a restart restores the original captured blocks.

A dry or blocked rise is ignored so a land spawn does not vanish. Demo sails are still ordinary ships: disassemble puts the oak, mast, and wool back; kill does not.

## What it means for you

Spawn a demo when you want to feel wind without building. Assemble when you already have a hull. Inspect is the only way to see whether the cloth is catching air — a sail edge-on to the wind can show a zero force even though the wind line is printed.

Cannons fire only for the owner or an operator, and only from that captured button. There is no powder to keep track of; if the shot does not go, you are either on cooldown or not allowed.

## Command reference

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/arch sail` | Spawn a medium sail hull three blocks ahead |
| `/arch sail small\|medium\|large` | Spawn a named-size hull |
| `/arch sail mesh` | Spawn a medium 3D cloth volume |
| `/arch sail <size> mesh` | Spawn a named-size 3D cloth volume |
